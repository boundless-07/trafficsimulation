Smart City Traffic Control

Four one-way lanes, one signal that runs itself (adaptive or fixed timing), a route finder with twelve locations, and a parking lot at every location, and an incident desk. Every part runs on a queue, a priority queue or a stack.

All four lanes are one-way and lead to one signal. A new car joins whichever of Lanes 1 to 3 has the fewest vehicles. Lane 4 is only for ambulances, fire trucks and buses and works as a priority queue: Ambulance first, then Fire truck, then Bus. When the signal is green, every lane moves forward.

      Activity

        Parking at every location

        Each location has its own lot with 3 bays and its own waiting queue. When a lot is full, new arrivals join its queue. When a vehicle leaves, the first vehicle in that queue takes the free bay.

        Activity

          Incident desk

          Reported incidents join a FIFO queue: the oldest report is dispatched first. A free patrol unit takes the front of the queue. When a unit resolves its incident, the incident is pushed onto a stack, so the latest resolved case is on top. "Reopen last" pops that stack and puts the incident at the back of the queue again.
